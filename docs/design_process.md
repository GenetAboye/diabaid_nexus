# User-Centered Design Process

DiabAid Nexus was developed using a user-centered approach.

The development process included:

1. Identification of barriers and needs related to diabetes self-management
2. Engagement with patients and healthcare professionals (to identify challenges and needs of people living with diabetes in self-management)
3. Translation of user and clinical requirements into application features
4. Iterative mobile application development
5. Usability evaluation with intended users
6. Refinement based on user feedback

The goal was to develop an application that was technically functional while also
being relevant to the realities of diabetes management in Sub-Saharan Africa.
